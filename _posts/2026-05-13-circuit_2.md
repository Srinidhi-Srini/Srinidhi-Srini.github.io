## Kirchhoff's Current Law (KCL)

Kirchhoff's Current Law (KCL) is a law that states that the total current entering a node is equal to the total current leaving a node:

$$ \sum_{}^{} I_{in} = \sum_{}^{} I_{out} $$

For sign convention, we can say current entering the node is positive, and leaving the node is negative.
